About Me

I transitioned into data analytics after realizing how much I enjoy uncovering patterns, solving complex problems, and turning data into clear stories that drive decisions. My background combines analytical thinking with a strong business mindset, allowing me to connect data insights directly to real‑world impact.

I work comfortably across the data lifecycle—from cleaning and transforming raw data to building dashboards and presenting insights to stakeholders. I have hands‑on experience with SQL, Excel, Python, Tableau, Power BI, and modern business intelligence workflows.

Beyond technical skills, I value clear communication, critical thinking, and collaboration. I enjoy working with cross‑functional teams, asking the right questions, and making sure insights are not only accurate but also understandable and actionable.
