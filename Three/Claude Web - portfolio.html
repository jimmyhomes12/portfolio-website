01 — about me

Data-driven by passion,
analytical by nature.

My journey into data analytics began with a curiosity for the "why" behind the numbers. What started as building Excel models to track team performance evolved into a deep love for SQL queries, Python scripts, and interactive dashboards that tell compelling stories.

I transitioned from operations management, where I saw firsthand how data-driven decisions outperformed gut instinct every time. That experience gave me a unique perspective — I understand not just the technical analysis, but the business context that makes insights actionable.

Today I specialize in end-to-end analytics: from data wrangling and cleaning in Python, to building complex SQL pipelines, to crafting executive dashboards in Tableau and Power BI that non-technical stakeholders can immediately act upon.

I pride myself on being a strong communicator — translating data findings into plain language, collaborating cross-functionally, and asking the right questions before diving into the data.
