01 — about me

Data-driven by passion,
analytical by nature.

My path into data analytics wasn't a straight line — it started with a relentless curiosity for the "why" behind numbers. Working in operations management, I watched teams make costly decisions based on gut instinct when the answers were sitting right there in the data. That frustration became my fuel.

I taught myself SQL and Python evenings and weekends, starting with small internal dashboards and growing into full end-to-end analytics pipelines. What once took a team days of manual Excel work, I was automating in hours — and stakeholders actually started asking for the data before making decisions.

Today I specialize in translating messy, real-world datasets into clear business intelligence. I've built customer segmentation models, revenue dashboards, churn predictors, and market analyses across e-commerce, SaaS, and retail. What I'm most proud of isn't the code — it's the moment a stakeholder says "I didn't know that."

I believe the best data analysts are also strong communicators. I present findings to C-suite and technical teams alike, always tailoring the narrative to the audience and grounding every chart in a decision that needs to be made.
